About Me

I'm a Graduate Research Assistant at the Human Centered Robotics Lab @ UT Austin, pursuing my M.S. in Mechanical Engineering with a focus on Robotics. My research centers on full-stack humanoid robotics development—from mechanical design and hardware integration to real-time control systems and learning-based morphology optimization.

Featured Research: DRACO 4 Humanoid Development

Leading the end-to-end development of DRACO 4, a next-generation humanoid platform designed to be maintainable, modular, well-documented, and robust. Using Isaac Lab and PyTorch, I'm exploring learning-based approaches to optimize humanoid morphology, with a particular focus on oblique lower body axis configurations.

Key Technologies: ROS2/EtherCAT, Isaac Lab, PyTorch, Mujoco, full-stack robotics
