About Me

I'm a Graduate Research Assistant at the Human Centered Robotics Lab @ UT Austin, pursuing my M.S. in Mechanical Engineering with a focus on Robotics.

My research centers on full-stack humanoid robotics development—from mechanical design and hardware integration to real-time control systems and learning-based morphology optimization.

I'm currently leading the development of DRACO 4, a next-generation humanoid platform designed to be maintainable, modular, well-documented, and robust. Using Isaac Lab and PyTorch, I'm exploring learning-based approaches to optimize humanoid morphology, with a particular focus on oblique lower body axis configurations.

My work spans the entire robotics stack: CAD design and fabrication, ROS2/EtherCAT real-time communication, simulation in Mujoco and Isaac Lab, and reinforcement learning for control and morphology optimization.
